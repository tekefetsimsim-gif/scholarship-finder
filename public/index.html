What this finder checks

📅 Application dates

Searches by application opening date in the current month and the next two months. Earlier-opening applications may also appear if they remain open.

🎓 Scholarships

🌍 Countries

🗣️ English requirements

Prioritises MOI acceptance and possible English waivers while keeping IELTS/TOEFL opportunities visible.

💰 Application fees

Separates no-fee applications, fee waivers, unknown fees and applications with reported fees.

🔎 Multiple sources

Searches across university websites, scholarship providers and other scholarship information sources.
